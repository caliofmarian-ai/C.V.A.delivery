# P001 — TikTok Content Test Plan

Product concept: Pet Hair Rescue Kit

## Core message

Pet hair on the sofa, clothes, bedding and car seats is a repeated everyday problem.

The content must show the result visually. Avoid unsupported performance claims.

## First 10 angles

1. **The black-sofa test**
   - Hook: “If you have a cat, you know what this sofa looks like after one day.”
   - Show one half cleaned, one half untouched.
   - CTA: “Would you use this instead of sticky rollers?”

2. **Vacuum vs roller**
   - Hook: “The vacuum looked finished. It wasn’t.”
   - Vacuum first, then reveal remaining embedded hair with the tool.
   - Do not claim superiority on all surfaces.

3. **One week of pet hair**
   - Hook: “This is what my sofa collected in seven days.”
   - Use a visible collection chamber.

4. **Black clothes before leaving**
   - Hook: “Five minutes before leaving the house…”
   - Fast practical use case.

5. **Car seat test**
   - Hook: “Dog owners: check your back seat.”
   - Demonstrate on fabric car seat.

6. **Sticky roller cost comparison**
   - Hook: “I was throwing these sheets away every week.”
   - Show reusable alternative.
   - Avoid environmental claims that cannot be substantiated.

7. **Three tools, three surfaces**
   - Hook: “One product does not work everywhere.”
   - Roller for couch, glove for fabric/curves, brush for narrow areas.

8. **Partner challenge**
   - Hook: “He said the sofa was already clean.”
   - Challenge/reaction format.

9. **Satisfying close-up**
   - Hook: no spoken hook; strong macro visual of hair collection.
   - ASMR/satisfaction format.

10. **Pet owner gift angle**
    - Hook: “The useful gift no cat owner asks for until they try it.”
    - Demonstrate, do not over-sell.

## First recommended video

**Before/after sofa split test**

Structure:
- 0–2s: visible hairy sofa + hook
- 2–6s: three fast passes
- 6–9s: split-screen result
- 9–12s: open collection chamber
- 12–15s: creator reaction + CTA

## Test variables

Change only one major variable at a time:
- hook
- first frame
- creator voice vs text-only
- video length
- offer/price
- CTA

## Metrics

Track:
- views
- 2-second hold
- completion rate
- product clicks
- click-through rate
- add-to-cart where available
- orders
- GMV
- commission
- comments by objection/theme
- refunds/returns where visible

## Interpretation

High views + low clicks:
- creative curiosity without product intent
- weak CTA
- poor product-audience fit

Clicks + no orders:
- price/offer problem
- trust problem
- listing/page problem
- shipping problem

Orders + weak profit:
- cost structure problem

Repeated negative comments:
- investigate exact complaint before scaling.
