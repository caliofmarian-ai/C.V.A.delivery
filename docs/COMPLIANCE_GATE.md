# Compliance Gate — Germany / EU

This gate is applied before a candidate can become an approved test product.

## Required checks

1. Exact SKU identified.
2. Supplier and manufacturer traceable.
3. EU responsible-person information available when required.
4. Product safety requirements checked.
5. CE/conformity requirements checked only where applicable to the product category.
6. Required warnings/instructions and language obligations checked.
7. Germany packaging/EPR route checked.
8. WEEE/battery obligations checked for relevant products.
9. TikTok Shop prohibited/restricted-product rules checked.
10. Trademark, copyright and counterfeit exposure checked.
11. Consumer return/warranty exposure assessed.
12. Exact fulfilment route and landed cost into Germany calculated.
13. VAT/customs treatment reviewed for the actual business/fulfilment structure.

## Outcomes

- PASS
- PASS WITH CONDITIONS
- LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
- RISK FLAG
- REJECT

## Principle

Do not infer compliance from a supplier badge, marketplace listing or product popularity.

Compliance evidence must refer to the exact product/SKU and actual route to market.
