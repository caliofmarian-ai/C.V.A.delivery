# Catalog Schema

Each product record should contain, where available:

## Identity
- product name
- internal product ID
- category
- niche
- status
- score
- confidence

## Supplier
- supplier name
- source/platform
- supplier URL/reference
- warehouse country
- manufacturer identity
- EU responsible person when required
- available variants
- minimum order quantity

## Logistics
- destination market
- product cost
- shipping cost
- shipping method
- estimated delivery time
- package weight/dimensions
- EU warehouse availability

## Market
- observed competitor prices
- recommended price
- demand signals
- trend signals
- review signals
- recurring customer complaints
- competition level

## Economics
- product cost
- shipping
- relevant duties/taxes
- platform/app costs
- processing fees
- commission
- expected return cost
- advertising cost
- real estimated cost
- profit/order
- margin %
- break-even
- maximum acceptable CPA
- minimum ROAS
- maximum CAC
- profit at 10 orders
- profit at 100 orders
- profit at 1,000 orders

## TikTok
- TikTok potential
- visual demonstration strength
- content angles
- hooks
- CTAs
- scripts
- LIVE ideas
- buyer objections

## Commercial development
- bundle opportunities
- upsell
- cross-sell
- differentiation
- brand potential
- repeat-purchase potential

## Risk
- legal/compliance status
- certification/conformity requirements
- IP/trademark risk
- platform-policy risk
- product-safety risk
- return risk
- warranty exposure

## Evidence discipline

Every important figure should preserve its source and observation date.

Unknown fields must be marked:
- DATA INSUFFICIENT
- NEVALIDATED
- LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
- RISK FLAG

Never fabricate a missing value.
