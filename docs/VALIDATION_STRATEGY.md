# Validation Strategy

## Default path

Whenever the product is available through TikTok Shop Affiliate, validate demand there before building a full Shopify offer.

AFFILIATE DISCOVERY
→ SAMPLE / CREATOR TEST
→ CONTENT TEST
→ CLICK / GMV DATA
→ CUSTOMER SIGNALS
→ SUPPLIER CHECK
→ SHOPIFY UNIT ECONOMICS
→ OWN OFFER
→ SCALE / HOLD / KILL

## Why affiliate first

Affiliate testing can validate:
- content-product fit
- hook strength
- click intent
- actual conversions
- audience-product fit

before the project assumes:
- direct fulfilment responsibility
- customer service exposure
- returns exposure
- Shopify subscription/app costs
- supplier stock risk

## Germany / EU creator onboarding

Current TikTok Shop documentation for Germany confirms an Affiliate programme for creators and requires identity/KYC and a valid payout method.

The exact account eligibility status must always be checked inside the creator's TikTok account because platform rules can change and account-specific restrictions may apply.

## Rule

Do not create the business or payout account under Marian's identity.

The client/creator is the business owner and must complete identity, KYC, bank and platform authorisation steps personally when required.
