# C.V.A.delivery

Intelligent product research, validation and ecommerce operating system for a Germany-based TikTok creator.

## Mission

Build a small, evidence-driven catalog of products with real potential for TikTok + Shopify + dropshipping, prioritizing profitability, logistics, compliance, repeatable content and brand potential over catalog size.

## Operating funnel

TikTok Audience → TikTok Content → TikTok Shop / Affiliate → Product Testing → Winning Products → Shopify → Dropshipping → Data → AI Analysis → Scaling → Private Label / Brand

## Core rule

We do not chase products because they are popular. A product enters testing only when demand, TikTok fit, unit economics, logistics, compliance and customer risk are sufficiently supported by evidence.

## Canonical statuses

- HIGH PRIORITY
- RAPID TEST
- WATCHLIST
- HOLD
- REJECTED
- NEVALIDATED
- DATA INSUFFICIENT
- RISK FLAG
- LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
- BRAND CANDIDATE

## Business ownership

The creator/client is the sole business owner. Marian assists the owner without charging a fee. Shopify, affiliate, supplier and other business accounts must use the owner's own identity and dedicated business email once supplied.
