# Research Batch 001 — Evidence Update — 22 Sep 2026

## Main finding

The reusable pet-hair-removal category has a real current Germany demand signal, but the generic single-roller offer is highly commoditised.

The stronger working hypothesis is a **Pet Hair Rescue Kit** rather than an undifferentiated single-product listing.

## Evidence summary

### Germany

Recent German market/review sources:
- Idealo observed ACE2ACE-style reusable roller offers around EUR 15.29–20.99.
- F.A.Z. Kaufkompass September 2026 test reports good cleaning performance but flags difficult handling on some surfaces and cleaning/jamming limitations.
- German comparison/review sources report over 100,000 customer reviews for ACE2ACE-type products.

### TikTok / social commerce

Current TikTok Shop product pages in another market show meaningful sold counts for reusable pet-hair tools, including gloves and brushes.

A January 2026 pet-hair-removal glove video indexed publicly accumulated approximately 2.7M views.

These are content-format signals, not Germany-specific conversion evidence.

### Customer problem validation

Recent community discussions continue to recommend ChomChom-style rollers for sofas and furniture.

Recurring limitations:
- surface dependence
- deeply embedded or long hair
- mechanism jams
- generic versions vary greatly in quality
