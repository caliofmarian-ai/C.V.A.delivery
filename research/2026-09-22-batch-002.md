## P002 key finding

The shoe-washing-bag category has a strong social-commerce signal.

One current TikTok Shop listing has roughly 50K+ sold, while German comparison sources show established retail availability from roughly EUR 8 upward, including Vileda.

However, product quality is a serious selection criterion. Reviews repeatedly mention zipper, seam and fiber failures.

The business should therefore compete on:
- product quality
- 2-pack / bundle
- clear shoe-care guidance
- trustworthy content

rather than simply price.

## P003 key finding

Car seat gap organizers have obvious viral-demo potential but are too commoditised for current priority.

Germany comparison pages show thousands of competing variants, and fit variability creates avoidable return risk.

## P004 key finding

Compression packing cubes remain commercially credible, with strong 2026 travel-content fit and low compliance complexity.

They are not urgent enough to outrank P001/P002 without audience evidence.
