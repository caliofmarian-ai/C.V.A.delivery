## Product hypothesis

A reusable chenille/microfiber shoe-washing bag with:
- secure/self-locking zipper
- dense internal cleaning fibers
- enough room for one pair or one shoe per bag, depending on design
- clear care instructions
- optional 2-pack configuration

Preferred positioning:

**A controlled way to machine-wash compatible textile/synthetic sneakers while reducing direct banging and loose movement in the drum.**

Do not claim:
- safe for every shoe
- safe for every washing machine
- guaranteed stain removal
- suitable for leather/suede or shoes whose manufacturer forbids machine washing

## Main product risks

Observed recurring complaints across reviews:
- zipper failure
- seams or bag material tearing
- lint/fiber shedding
- possible colour transfer
- machine imbalance with heavy footwear
- poor quality on cheap versions

The physical sample gate is therefore mandatory.

## Shoe-compatibility risk

Nike Germany explicitly does not recommend washing its sneakers in a washing machine.

Other German appliance/retail guidance notes that some textile/synthetic shoes may be machine-washable with gentle settings, while leather, embellished, structured or manufacturer-restricted footwear may not be suitable.

Therefore the product page and content must always instruct the user to:
1. check the shoe manufacturer's care instructions
2. remove loose debris
3. use a gentle cycle where appropriate
4. avoid unsupported shoe materials/styles
5. air dry unless the shoe manufacturer states otherwise
