## Rejected sourcing behaviour

Do not:
- order 50–500 units because the price looks cheap
- select a supplier only from product photography
- trust “all shoes” or “washer & dryer safe” marketing without verifying the exact use limitations
- put a custom logo on the first untested sample

## Recommended sequence

1. Obtain one unbranded sample from Candidate A or another true sample-MOQ supplier.
2. Test durability repeatedly.
3. Compare against one higher-sales reference SKU.
4. Validate TikTok Affiliate demand.
5. Only if product + demand pass, request custom-logo sample from Candidate C.
6. Use Candidate B as a scale-price/quality benchmark, not as the first bulk order.
